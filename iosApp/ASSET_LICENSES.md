# iOS asset notes

The original images under `Resources/Images` were selected from the local kit at
`reffffs/receitas da vovo kit` and renamed to ASCII-safe filenames for Xcode.
They are bundled locally; the app does not fetch remote artwork or send image
data anywhere. Confirm final commercial usage rights for the original kit before
public App Store submission.

## Owner-provided components used for the 1.0.1 review

The 40 files named `component_*.png` are resized derivatives of the supplied
dish, ingredient, utensil, and interface artwork. Only optimized transparent
PNG copies are bundled in `Resources/Images/`. This records their project
provenance; it does not assign or establish usage rights for the supplied
components.
